Formulas and limitations

  • Click rate = clicked / sent · Report rate = reported / sent
  • Open tracking is limited: opens are usually measured with an invisible "tracking pixel". Many email clients block images or preload them, so open numbers are never exact (they can be too low or too high).
  • No passwords are stored: the credential-harvest simulation is a non-functional training form — only the fact that it was submitted is recorded.
  • One click is not a verdict: results are for education, not for labelling people as "dangerous".
