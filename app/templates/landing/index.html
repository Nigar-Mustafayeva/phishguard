ETHICAL BY DESIGN

Built to educate, never to punish

PhishGuard is meant for authorised, controlled environments. Results exist to target training — not to label people as “dangerous”.

No credentials stored Credential-harvest simulations only record that a form was submitted — never what was typed.
Safe demo mode The public demo never sends real email; synthetic results are clearly labelled.
Secrets stay server-side API keys live in environment variables and are never sent to the browser.
Full audit trail Logins, launches and report downloads are recorded for accountability.
