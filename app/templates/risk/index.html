How is the risk score calculated?

  • The click ratio is the main factor — every campaign where the person clicked raises the score.
  • Entering data into the training form raises the score further.
  • Repeat clicks across several campaigns add extra points.
  • Reporting the suspicious email lowers the score (positive behaviour).
  • Scores exist to target training — not to label people as "dangerous".
